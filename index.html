WORLD Trade Everything

Independent creative work exploring World, the on-chain prediction market. The identity, product and positioning are theirs; my work builds on that foundation through a series of key visuals, films and social concepts created for X. The creative approach was to translate an almost unbounded product idea into something calm, precise and inevitable, designed to stand apart in a feed built for noise. Selected work was reposted by @world_xyz, with some pieces further developed following direct creative feedback from the team.

The creative premise

The bottleneck moves from permission to proof.

World’s premise is that every exchange ever built is limited by listing. A committee decides what may be traded, where, and during which hours. On-chain there is only one question: can this be resolved by an oracle? The work translates that idea into a feed, without turning it into hype.

BUILT On My Own

Since 2021 I have been on parental leave, caring for my son. I used the time to stop handing designs over and to build them myself. Kotlin, Jetpack Compose and the modern web stack, all self-taught. One app is live on the Play Store, an AI product went from idea to live in four days. Every one of these is finished and running. Nothing here is a mockup.

01Live on Play Store

SplitEasy

Android · solo

Bill splitting with on-device receipt scanning: point the camera at a receipt, OCR reads the items, the app splits them. Product, interface, code, store listing, monetisation and consent flow, all of it alone.

KotlinJetpack ComposeMaterial 3RoomCameraXML Kit OCRAdMob + UMPR8

Module 01

Interactive learning · ~8 min

A working e-learning module built as a job application, because describing instructional design in a PDF proves nothing. Five chapters, branching scenarios, an xAPI statement fired on every interaction, and a live tracking panel.

03Live

Mast

AI product · 4 days

You tie a note to an emotional state instead of a date; months later it recognises you are back in that state and returns your own words. Ships with a forced response schema, a fail-closed parser and a browser-side fallback so the demo still completes when the model is unreachable.

Personal finance for people who are not accountants. Privacy is the architecture rather than a policy: no accounts, no sync, no telemetry, encrypted local storage, ads forced to non-personalised with no advertising ID. 94 Kotlin source files against a phased roadmap.
